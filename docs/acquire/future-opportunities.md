# Future Opportunities

## Revenue Models

- Team subscription tiers (Starter, Growth, Elite) based on seat count and feature depth.
- Paid AI analysis credits for high-volume video processing.
- Compliance and audit exports as a premium add-on.
- White-label licensing for recruiting agencies and private training organizations.

## Feature Expansion Ideas

- Automated outreach cadences with compliance-safe message templates.
- AI board recommendations and fit scoring by position archetype.
- Coach collaboration timelines and decision attribution analytics.
- Integration marketplace (Hudl, MaxPreps, calendar/communications suites).
- Portfolio-style athlete dossiers for sharing with admissions/staff.

## Market Positioning

ScoutVision can position as:

- "The operating system for modern recruiting staffs"
- "CRM + compliance + AI scouting in one platform"
- "A buyer-ready foundation for sports-tech expansion"

## Expansion Path

- Near-term: deepen college recruiting workflows and retention tooling.
- Mid-term: grow into club/academy markets with lighter pricing tiers.
- Long-term: data network effects from cross-program benchmarking and predictive models.
